About me

A calmer path to moving again.

I am a neuro physiotherapist with a master's degree in Neuroscience Physiotherapy. My work centres on helping people relearn everyday movement — standing, walking, balancing and using their hands — after stroke, nerve injury and other neurological conditions.

I also treat children, older adults, women's health concerns, and orthopaedic, cardiorespiratory and post-operative conditions. Every plan begins with a detailed assessment and includes exercises you can do at home.
